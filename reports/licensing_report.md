## 2. Platform Compliance and Terms of Service Adherence

- **No Bypass of Authentication:** Zero rate-limit evasion, scrapers violating `robots.txt`, or credential cracking was executed. Where platforms required OAuth or user tokens (e.g., Kaggle API credentials), the platform boundary was documented and legitimate open repositories were prioritized.
- **No Private Medical Records / PII:** Healthcare subtopics (Domain 16 and Domain 20) utilize public medical glossaries, diagnostic criteria summaries, and nutritional labeling standards from verified institutions (e.g., NIH, CDC, WHO reference compendia). No identifiable patient health information (PHI) is present.
- **Exclusion of Pirated Material:** In Domains 21 to 25 (Cinema, Television, Books), copyrighted commercial scripts, unauthorized television video streams, or subscription-gated ebooks were strictly avoided. Public-domain literary texts (Project Gutenberg) and encyclopedia synopses were utilized.
