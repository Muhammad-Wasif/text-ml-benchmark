[[File:The Girl Guides Association in Britain, 1914-1918 Q27921.jpg|thumb|[[Girl guides]] cleaning a room, 1917]]'''Housekeeping''' is the management and routine support activities of running and maintaining an organized physical institution occupied or used by people, like a [[house]], [[ship]], [[hospital]], [[business]] or [[factory]]. The term may refer to [[cleaning]], [[cooking]], organization, [[shopping]], and bill payment. The term is also used to refer to the money allocated for such use.<ref name="oxford2">[https://web.archive.org/web/20140408224243/http://www.oxforddictionaries.com/definition/english/housekeeping?q=housekeeping "housekeeping"] ''[[Oxford Dictionary of English|Oxford Dictionaries Online]]''. Retrieved 2 June 2013.</ref> These tasks may be performed by members of the [[household]], or by [[Domestic worker|persons hired for the purpose]]. In the United States, the term is associated with institutional housekeeping at [[Hotel|hotels]] and [[Resort|resorts]] but may also refer to domestic housekeeping.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Jones|first=Thomas J. A.|url=https://books.google.com/books?hl=en&lr=&id=-6HPXYDI7yYC&oi=fnd&pg=PP1&dq=housekeeping&ots=Nf2IVSDZ4t&sig=fp5rhXE1n-6peIcygPBzMNW4S58#v=onepage&q=housekeeping&f=false|title=Professional Management of Housekeeping Operations|date=2007-10-26|publisher=John Wiley & Sons|isbn=978-0-471-76244-7|language=en}}</ref> Housekeeper is a broader role than a [[cleaner]], who is focused only on the cleaning aspect.<ref name="House Keeper vs Cleaner differentiation2">{{cite web |date=21 December 2017|title=What's the Difference Between Housekeeping and Cleaning|url=https://www.thinkacw.com.au/blog/hospitality/whats-the-difference-between-housekeeping-and-cleaning/|website=ThinkACW}}</ref> The term may also refer to the [[Housekeeping (computing)|maintenance]] of [[Computer data storage|computer storage]] systems, records, etc.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Scott|first=Ralph L.|date=2009-01-21|title=Wired to the World: Computer Housekeeping|url=https://www.ncl.ecu.edu/index.php/NCL/article/view/216|journal=North Carolina Libraries|language=en|volume=60|issue=4|pages=116|doi=10.3776/ncl.v60i4.216|issn=2379-4305|doi-access=free}}</ref><ref>[https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/housekeeping "housekeeping"] ''[[The_Collins_English_Dictionary]]''. Retrieved 2 June 2013.</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=April 5, 2011 |title=AE MISC PUB 25-400-2 |url=https://www.aepubs.eur.army.mil/AE-Misc/ |website=Army in Europe and Africa Publications (AEAPUBS)}}</ref>

A [[Housekeeper (domestic worker)|housekeeper]] is a person employed to manage a household<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20121204143020/http://oxforddictionaries.com/definition/english/housekeeper "housekeeper"] ''[[Oxford Dictionary of English|Oxford Dictionaries Online]]''. Retrieved 2 June 2013.</ref> and the [[domestic staff]]. According to the 1861 Victorian era ''[[Mrs. Beeton's Book of Household Management]]'', the housekeeper is second in command in the house and "except in large establishments, where there is a house steward, the housekeeper must consider herself as the immediate representative of her mistress".<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20080729181104/http://ebooks.adelaide.edu.au/b/beeton/isabella/household/chapter2.html#chapter2 ''Mrs. Beeton's Book of Household Management'']  Web version of the book at the [[University_of_Adelaide]] Library. Retrieved 2 June 2013.</ref>

'''Domestic housekeeping''' refers to housekeeping by or for private households, while '''institutional housekeeping''' for commercial and other institutions providing shelter or lodging, such as hotels, resorts, inns, boarding houses, dormitories, hospitals and prisons. Additional concepts include '''workplace housekeeping''' and '''industrial housekeeping''', which are part of occupational health and safety processes.

==Scope==

=== Domestic housekeeping ===
{{see also|Homemaking}}
'''Domestic housekeeping''' or '''residential housekeeping''' refers to the cleaning and maintaining of a private residence, home or apartment. This can include tasks such as dusting, vacuuming, laundry, and disinfecting surfaces. It may also involve organizing and decluttering living spaces, as well as making beds and changing linens. This type of housekeeping is sometimes performed by a [[Cleaning service|professional cleaning service]] or by the homeowners themselves. The frequency of housekeeping tasks may vary depending on the size of the residence, the number of occupants, and individual preferences.
[[File:Pepe passant l'aspiradora (País Valencià, 2023).jpg|thumb|258x258px|A man vacuums a floor, a common housekeeping task]]

=== Professional housekeeping ===
Professional cleaning services may offer a variety of services, depending on the needs and expectations of the clients. Some of the most common types are:

* '''Regular domestic cleaning''' is performed on a regular basis (e.g., weekly, biweekly, or monthly). The main purpose of this service is to maintain cleanliness in the home. A cleaner usually follows a list of tasks that cover the most important and frequently used areas of the home, such as the kitchen, bathroom, living room, and bedroom.
* '''One-off domestic cleanings''' or '''one-time domestic cleanings''' are performed only once, usually for a specific occasion or purpose (e.g., after a party, before moving in or after moving out, after a renovation, or following a large event in the home). The main purpose of this service is to restore the original condition and appearance of the home.
* '''Specialized domestic cleanings''' focus on a particular aspect or area of the home that requires extra attention or expertise. This might refer to carpet- or oven cleaning, upholstery cleaning, end-of-tenancy cleaning, or [[crime scene cleanup]]. The cleaners usually use specialized equipment and products to perform these tasks.

[[File:Hotel Horizonte 02.jpg|thumb|293x293px|A hotel housekeeper cleaning a bathroom mirror]]

===Institutional housekeeping===

* At commercial lodging establishments ([[Hotel|hotels]], [[Resort|resorts]], [[Hostel|hostels]], [[Cruise ship|cruise ships]], etc.) housekeeping refers to the department which works to provide a clean, comfortable, safe and aesthetically appealing environment for the guests. It also refers to the operational department in a hotel is responsible for these activities in relation to private rooms, public areas, [[Elevator|elevators]] and [[Staircase|stairwells]]. This includes changing [[bedding]], providing clean [[Towel|towels]], cleaning of [[Bathroom|bathrooms]] and tidying of the room.<ref>{{Cite book |last=مصطفى،|first=يوسف،|title=إدارة الإشراف الداخلي في الفنادق = Housekeeping department in hotel|year=2016|isbn=978-9957-32-938-9|edition=الطبعة الأولى|language=ar|trans-title=Housekeeping department in hotel}}</ref> This department is typically in charge of laundry services.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Casado|first=Matt A.|url=https://books.google.com/books?hl=en&lr=&id=AsU9EQAAQBAJ&oi=fnd&pg=PR13&dq=housekeeping+practices+in+hotel&ots=scotlXZSYI&sig=YyiTjXpV4IdNtPwXA-LXUUsj7dg#v=onepage&q=housekeeping%20practices%20in%20hotel&f=false|title=Housekeeping Management|date=2011-10-04|publisher=John Wiley & Sons|isbn=978-1-118-07179-3|language=en}}</ref> The housekeeping department does not directly generate income, and is considered a "[[Front of house|back of house]]" department despite the limited direct contact with the guests.
* In communal lodging and living situations ([[Prison|prisons]], [[dormitory]], [[barracks]], [[House share|house shares]], etc.) housekeeping may be organized formally or informally to ensure the cleanliness and safety of common spaces.<ref>{{Cite web |date=October 8, 1982|title=TM 5-609 - Military custodial services manual|url=https://everyspec.com/ARMY/TM-Tech-Manual/TM_5-609_CHG-2_5059/|website=Departments of the Army and Navy}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2017-11-16|title=Housekeeping a vital part of safety program|url=https://www.army.mil/article/197041/housekeeping_a_vital_part_of_safety_program|access-date=2026-05-19|website=www.army.mil|language=en}}</ref>
* Workplace and industrial housekeeping, sometimes called [[occupational safety and health]], is the ongoing process of keeping the workplace clean, hygienic, orderly and free of extraneous objects and materials which may constitute [[Hazard|hazards]] (e.g., [[Body fluid|body fluids]], unsafe placement of items, etc.). This may include consideration of a buildings layout, safety markings, removing clutter from storage facilities, maintenance of support infrastructure, lighting, and regular inspections, and is a basic component of fire and incident prevention. In [[hospitals]] and clinics, housekeeping may refer to either the department responsible for cleanliness of patient rooms, maintenance and aesthetic upkeep of patient care areas, public areas and staff areas.<ref name="CCOHS22">{{cite web |title=Workplace Housekeeping - Basic Guide|url=https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/hsprograms/house.html|access-date=11 October 2022|website=www.ccohs.ca|publisher=Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety: Government of Canada}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Sjölund|first=Erik|title=How industrial housekeeping contributes to making workplaces better and safer|url=https://www.nederman.com/en/knowledge-center/industrial-housekeeping|access-date=11 October 2022|website=www.nederman.com}}</ref>

== Components of housekeeping ==
[[File:A_Day_in_the_Life_of_a_Wartime_Housewife-_Everyday_Life_in_London,_England,_1941_D2366.jpg|thumb|A London housewife clears [[ash]] from [[Fireplace|the grate]], 1941]]Housekeeping is synonymous with '''housecleaning'''. The term ''housecleaning'' is often used also figuratively in politics and business, for the removal of unwanted personnel, methods, or policies in an effort at reform or improvement.<ref>[http://www.thefreedictionary.com/housecleaning ''The American Heritage Dictionary of the English Language''] Retrieved 2011-01-23</ref> Literally, it may refer to activities like disposing of [[Garbage|rubbish]], cleaning dirty surfaces, dusting, and vacuuming. It may also involve some outdoor chores, such as removing leaves from rain gutters, washing windows, and sweeping doormats.

Concerns of the housekeeper may vary widely from place to place, both domestically and institutionally. Domestically and regionally chores have changed over time as new technologies have been adopted. For instance, the task of clearing of [[ash]] from [[fireplaces]] and [[Ashtray|ashtrays]] have become less common. Many housekeeping classes around the world once taught the [[sewing]] or [[mending]] of clothes.<ref>{{Cite book |last=BUNINA|first=O.F.|title=Домоводство : Учебник для V-VII классов|last2=NECHIPORUK|first2=3.S.|publisher=Ministry of Education of the Ukrainian SSR|year=1961|language=ru|trans-title=Home Economics: Textbook for Grades V-VII}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book |last=Fuller|first=Alice|url=https://archive.org/details/housekeeping-and-household-arts/page/n7/mode/2up|title=Housekeeping and Household Arts|publisher=[[Department of Education (Philippines)]]|year=1911}}</ref> Fewer hotels now offer whole room [[Disinfectant|disinfection]] after the peak of the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Butuhy|first=Cláudia Regina Rodrigues Sarralheiro|last2=Luna|first2=Expedito José de Albuquerque|date=2026|title=The COVID-19 pandemic in the hotel industry: the application of hygiene and safety protocols by hotel employees|url=https://www.scielo.br/j/rimtsp/a/vnV6CVrMXNyz4sh9QmSXzGb/?lang=en|journal=Revista do Instituto de Medicina Tropical de São Paulo|language=en|volume=68|pages=e24|doi=10.1590/S1678-9946202668024|issn=1678-9946|pmc=12987604|pmid=41849550}}</ref>

=== Tidying ===
Tidying (sometimes called "decluttering") is an organization process that can include activities such as picking up and collecting items that are not in their proper storage and returning them to their allocated storage place.<ref>{{Cite web |date=April 30, 1943 |title=Streamlined housekeeping - Homemaker's Chat (USDA) |url=https://archive.org/search?query=external-identifier:%22urn:oclc:record:1157663331%22 |access-date=2026-05-19 |website=United States Department of Agriculture, Office of Information, Radio Service - archive.org}}</ref> This process reduces the risk of damage to the items and removed the hazard they may cause for tripping and obstructing other activities. It can involve removing items like [[Lock and key|keys]], [[Book|books]], [[Handbag|handbags]], or [[Remote control|remote controls]] from tables and surfaces and putting them in their correct place. Putting out-of-place items away creates a clear space which facilitates removing dirt buildup and accessing areas that are hard to reach and clean on a regular basis. It generally does not include moving large items of furniture around to access the surfaces under or behind them, but may include putting them back where they belong after cleaning.<ref name="House Tidying">{{cite web |title=Is It Normal To Have a Messy House? |url=https://www.capitalhire.com.au/is-it-normal-to-have-a-messy-house/ |website=Capital Hire Skips|date=21 September 2021 }}</ref>

=== Removal of dirt and dust ===
[[File:Cleaning facilities.jpg|alt=Mops, brooms, and cleaning chemicals in bottles and bags|thumb|251x251px|Cleaning tools vary widely from place to place, but generally include mops, brooms, and cleaning agents. ]]
Over time [[dust]] accumulates on household surfaces and is considered unsightly. Various tools have been invented for dust removal like dust mops, [[Feather duster|feather dusters]], paper, cotton or polyester dust cloths, furniture sprays and [[Wax|waxes]], and vacuum cleaners. Vacuum cleaners often have a variety of tools to enable them to remove dirt not just from carpets and rugs, but also from hard surfaces and upholstery.<ref>Kennedy, Rose. 2006. 10-Minute Housekeeping. Beverly, MA: Fair Winds Press.</ref>
[[File:US_Army_51089_Unsung_heroes.jpg|thumb|Women cleaning a [[Cabin (housing)|cabin]] of the US Army]]
Surface cleaning can include vacuuming, scrubbing, sweeping, mopping, or disinfecting hard surfaces like floors, [[Countertop|countertops]], or tables, as well as furnishing textiles, carpeting and upholstery, cooking and eating utensils. Many [[Household chemicals|household]] and industrial chemicals can be used in the cleaning of an area. To ensure safety, protective apparel including rubber [[gloves]], [[Mask|face covers]], and [[Eye protection|protective eyewear]] are also sometimes used when dealing with chemical cleaning products.<ref>{{Cite web |title=OSHA requirements for cleaning chemicals. {{!}} Occupational Safety and Health Administration|url=https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/1990-01-29|access-date=2018-12-19|website=www.osha.gov}}</ref>

Disposal of rubbish is an important aspect of house cleaning. [[Bin bag|Plastic refuse bags]] are designed and manufactured specifically for the collection of refuse and are common in many countries, as is the reuse of plastic bags and containers for this purpose.<ref>Dellutri, Laura. 2005. Speed Cleaning 101. Meredith Books, Des Moines, Iowa.</ref>

===Laundry===
Housekeeping can include the washing, folding, and packing away laundry items. Other duties may involve monitoring and changing bed linen and ironing. This may be done domestically, in [[barracks]], or [[Hotel|hotels]].

===Tools===
Tools used in housecleaning include [[Vacuum cleaner|vacuums]], [[Broom|brooms]], [[Mop|mops]] and [[Sponge (material)|sponges]], together with cleaning products such as [[Detergent|detergents]], and [[Disinfectant|disinfectants]]. [[Broom|Brooms]] remove debris from floors and [[Dustpan|dustpans]] carry dust and debris swept into them, [[Bucket|buckets]] hold cleaning and rinsing solutions, [[Vacuum cleaner|vacuum cleaners]] and [[Carpet sweeper|carpet sweepers]] remove surface dust and debris, [[chamois leather]] and [[Squeegee|squeegees]] are used for window-cleaning, and [[Mop|mops]] are used for washing floors.<ref>Bredenberg, Jeff et al. 1998. ''Clean It Fast, Clean It Right.'' Emmaus, PA: Rodale</ref>

==Social significance==

While domestic housekeeping can be seen as an objective activity that can be done by either men or women, some people have argued that housekeeping is a site of historical oppression and gender division between men and women.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.feministezine.com/feminist/modern/The-Politics-of-Housework.html | title=The Politics of Housework | publisher=Redstockings | date=1970 | access-date=3 February 2015 | first= Pat| last= Mainardi|author-link=Patricia Mainardi}}</ref> Housekeeping also has a role in maintaining certain parts of the capitalist economy, including the division of home and work life, as well as industries that sell chemicals and household goods.

A survey conducted by the U.S. [[Bureau of Labor Statistics]] in 2014 came to the result that approximately 43 percent of men did food preparation or cleanup on any given day, compared with approximately 70 percent of women. In addition, 20 percent of men did housekeeping chores (including cleaning and laundry) on any given day, compared to approximately 50 percent of women.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.bls.gov/news.release/atus.nr0.htm|title=American Time Use Survey|date=June 24, 2015|publisher=[[Bureau of Labor Statistics]]}}</ref>
